# Dronaldo
## Platform: RP2040
## Hardware Revision: Shares Murci hardware
## Status: Workshop firmware (prototype)

A minimal-UI chord drone synth built for the mumunator workshop. Students take
home an always-sounding 5-voice Braids drone they can play with four buttons
and four knobs — no display, no encoder, no menus.

## Controls
| #  | Type | Primary Function    | Notes                                   |
|----|------|---------------------|-----------------------------------------|
| 1  | Pot  | Root                | Quantized to semitones, 2-octave range  |
| 2  | Pot  | Timbre              | Braids Wavetables morph                 |
| 3  | Pot  | Cutoff              | LP filter — full CCW = silence          |
| 4  | Pot  | Inversion           | -6..+6, center detent at 0              |
| 5  | Btn  | Major 7             | Selects chord quality (radio style)     |
| 6  | Btn  | Minor 7             | Selects chord quality                   |
| 7  | Btn  | Dominant 7          | Selects chord quality                   |
| 8  | Btn  | Half-diminished 7   | Selects chord quality                   |

## Pin Mapping
| Function      | MCU Pin | ADC Ch | Notes                            |
|---------------|---------|--------|----------------------------------|
| Root Pot      | 26      | 0      | Quantized to semitone            |
| Timbre Pot    | 27      | 1      | Braids timbre 0..32767           |
| Cutoff Pot    | 28      | 2      | SVF cutoff + silencer gate       |
| Inversion Pot | 29      | 3      | Quantized -6..+6                 |
| BTN Maj7      | 6       | —      | Active LOW (INPUT_PULLUP)        |
| BTN Min7      | 7       | —      | Active LOW                       |
| BTN Dom7      | 8       | —      | Active LOW                       |
| BTN ø7        | 9       | —      | Active LOW                       |
| I2S BCLK      | 2       | —      | LRCK inferred = 3                |
| I2S DIN       | 4       | —      | Audio data                       |

## Chord Intervals (semitones from root)
| Btn | Name  | Intervals       |
|-----|-------|-----------------|
| 0   | MAJ7  | 0, 4, 7, 11     |
| 1   | MIN7  | 0, 3, 7, 10     |
| 2   | DOM7  | 0, 4, 7, 10     |
| 3   | ø7    | 0, 3, 6, 10     |

A fifth voice always doubles the root +24 semitones for low-end body.

## Behavior Notes
- Output is continuous. Voices never retrigger — button presses and pot moves
  update pitches legato so there are no clicks.
- To change chords silently, close the cutoff pot fully, change buttons/root,
  then reopen. Bottom ~9% of the cutoff pot acts as a soft mute.
- Root pot spans MIDI 48–71 (C3–B4), quantized to 24 semitone bins with
  hysteresis to prevent boundary flicker.

## Firmware
| File             | Description                                      |
|------------------|--------------------------------------------------|
| Dronaldo.ino     | Main entry, audio timer, input loop              |
| config.h         | Pin map, chord table, tunable constants          |
| audio_engine.cpp | Braids oscillator + Chamberlin SVF filter        |
| audio_engine.h   | DroneVoice struct + SVF coefficient helpers      |

No sequencer, no persistence, no MIDI, no display. Single core (Core 0).
